Method and assumptions

Rectangular fabric yardage planner

Add two seam allowances to each finished piece dimension and divide by one minus the shrinkage fraction. Fit whole pieces across the usable bolt width, round the required rows up, and multiply by cut-piece length. Round the final fabric length up to the purchase increment. Rotation is considered only when explicitly allowed.

Limits and interpretation

This is a rectangular cutting layout, not a garment-pattern estimator. Keep rotation off when grain, nap or a directional print prevents it. The model applies the same shrinkage fraction in both directions. Pattern matching, bias cutting and fabric defects require an additional cutting plan.

Units and rounding

Lengths convert using exactly 0.3048 metres per foot and 2.54 centimetres per inch. Area and volume conversions use the corresponding squared and cubed factors. Counts round up where whole items must be purchased; wallpaper drops per roll and fabric pieces across a width round down. Displayed values may be rounded, while calculations retain additional precision.

Compare inputs with the worked examples on the calculator page. A different material, layout or measurement can require different assumptions.
