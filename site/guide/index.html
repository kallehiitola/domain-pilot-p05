How to work out how much fabric you need

Fabric for cushions, curtains, napkins, quilting blocks, and rectangular panels depends on how many pieces fit side-by-side across the usable width of the bolt—not just total square area.

Step-by-step measuring walkthrough

  1. Measure the finished width and length of each rectangular piece and count how many identical pieces you need.
  2. Add seam allowance to every stitched edge (commonly 1/2 in or 5/8 in; double-turned hems need extra width).
  3. Check the usable bolt width after subtracting the unfinished selvedge edges (typically 1 to 1.5 in total off the nominal bolt width).
  4. Add a pre-wash shrinkage percentage for natural fibers such as cotton or linen, and set the smallest increment your fabric store sells (often 0.25 yd or 0.1 m).
  5. Only allow 90-degree piece rotation if the fabric is non-directional and has no nap or grainline restriction.

What to enter in the calculator

  • Finished piece width: Finished dimension before adding seams.
  • Finished piece length: Finished dimension along the grain.
  • Number of pieces: Total identical rectangular pieces needed.
  • Seam allowance per edge: Common allowances: 0.5 in (1.3 cm) or 0.625 in (1.5 cm).
  • Usable bolt width: Subtract ~1 to 2 in for selvedges from nominal bolt width.
  • Shrinkage allowance: Typical pre-wash shrinkage: 3% to 5% for cotton or linen.
  • Store purchase increment: Smallest cut sold (typically 0.25 yd in US, 0.1 m metric).
  • Allow 90° piece rotation: Leave off for directional prints, stripes, or velvet nap.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using nominal bolt width (such as 45 in) instead of usable printed width (often 42 to 43 in inside the selvedges).
  • Dividing total piece area by bolt width, which ignores wasted strips along the edge when pieces do not divide evenly across the bolt.
  • Rotating pieces on velvet, corduroy, one-way prints, or directional stripes, causing mismatched sheen or upside-down motifs.
  • Skipping pre-wash shrinkage on linen or unwashed cotton, which can shrink 3% to 8% in length.

Practical ordering checklist

  • Pre-wash and press natural fabrics before cutting whenever the finished item will be laundered.
  • Keep pieces aligned with the lengthwise grain for stability on cushions, aprons, and panels.
  • Add extra yardage if large motifs must be centered or matched across seams.
